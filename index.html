Arrow Escape: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026 · App ID: com.arrowscape.game

EnglishTürkçeالعربية

Summary

Arrow Escape is an offline puzzle game. It does not require an account and does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, settings, hint balance, daily challenge history and gameplay statistics are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Data we collect

None. The current version of the app has no internet access, shows no advertising, contains no analytics or tracking tools, and does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or advertising identifier. On Android, the only permission it requests is vibration, for optional haptic feedback.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that process data (for example optional advertising), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and any required consent will be requested in the app.
